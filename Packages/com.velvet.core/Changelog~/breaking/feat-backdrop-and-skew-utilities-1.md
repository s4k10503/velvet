### Changed

- A skew on both axes composes as Tailwind's `skewX() skewY()`: x is also stretched by 1 + tan θx · tan θy, where
  it was sheared as a single `skew(θx, θy)`. The face, a gradient on it, each child's seat and the overhang Velvet
  reserves under a filter all take that stretch, which is 4.5% under `skew-x-12 skew-y-12`. A skew on one axis is
  unchanged.
